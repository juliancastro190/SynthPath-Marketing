Before doing ANY marketing work (writing copy, an ad, an email, a sales or opt-in page, or planning a funnel) read `jareds-takes.md` first for the principles. Then read the files that fit the task: `the-fundamentals.md` for funnel strategy and structure, plus the specific playbook (copywriting, sales letter, email, ads, lead magnets, content, or analytics). Load that context before you write a single word.

## Files in this folder

- [[jareds-takes]] — 35 core principles. Always read first.
- [[the-fundamentals]] — the whole funnel: Content, Lead Magnet, Tripwire, Core Offer, Profit Maximizer.
- [[marketing-copywriting]] — copy, headlines, sales and opt-in pages.
- [[marketing-sales-letter]] — long-form sales letters, the 12-step structure.
- [[marketing-email]] — email, sequences, broadcasts.
- [[marketing-fb-ads]] — paid ads, Facebook and beyond.
- [[marketing-lead-magnets]] — lead magnets and opt-in offers.
- [[marketing-content]] — content marketing and the content library.
- [[marketing-analytics]] — analytics, metrics, what to measure.
- [[about]] — who jaredrhod is.
- [[the-thesis]] — why the fundamentals matter more than the tools.
